## 3. Mission & Core Business Scope

DOUITSU LLC operates as an officially licensed **Registered Support Organization (登録支援機関 - Touroku Shien Kikan)** under Japan's Immigration Control and Refugee Recognition Act. 

### Primary Business Objectives:
1. **Supporting Specified Skilled Workers (特定技能 1号 - SSW1)**:
   Ensuring foreign nationals working under the Tokutei Ginou visa reside and work smoothly, stably, and safely in Japan across their occupational, personal, and social lives.
2. **Assisting Japanese Host Companies (受入れ機関 / 受入れ企業)**:
   Taking full legal responsibility for creating, executing, and reporting the mandatory statutory support plan on behalf of Japanese employers. This reduces corporate administrative burden, prevents workplace compliance errors, and fosters long-term employee retention.
3. **Cross-Cultural Bridge (Japan ⇄ Myanmar / Global)**:
   Specializing in supporting Myanmar nationals transitioning into the Japanese workforce, offering comprehensive trilingual services in Japanese, Myanmar, and English.
